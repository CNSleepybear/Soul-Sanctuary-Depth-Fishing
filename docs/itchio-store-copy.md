## 🇬🇧 English Store Description

### Tagline (short description)

> A tiny desktop fishing roguelike widget. Cast lines into 12 pixelated global sanctuaries, collect 141 spirit fish, and build the ultimate Charm synergy during your coffee breaks.

---

### Description (full)

**Soul Sanctuary: Depth Fishing** is not a full-screen game — it's a **desktop companion**. A small, frameless window that floats in the corner of your screen while you work. Press `Ctrl+Shift+F`, cast a line into a beautifully pixelated low-poly sanctuary, and spend 20 seconds in a tense fishing mini-game before returning to your task.

### 🎮 What You'll Do

- **Cast & Reel**: A one-button tension mini-game. Hold to reel, release to slack. Master 6 fish behavior patterns (Steady, Sprint, Lazy, Zigzag, DeepDiver, SurfaceJumper).
- **Explore 12 Sanctuaries**: From Kyoto's cherry blossom shrine to Iceland's aurora fjord, from Parisian autumn rivers to a neon-drenched cyberpunk alley. Each sanctuary is a handcrafted voxel diorama.
- **Collect 141 Spirit Fish**: Fill your Fishpedia across 5 rarities (Common → Mythic). Each fish has unique 3D voxel model, behavior AI, and cultural lore.
- **Roguelike Runs**: Before each expedition, pick 3 Charms from 22. Stack Synergy tags. Earn permanent Relics. Survive random events. Cash out early or risk it all.

### ✨ Key Features

- 🖼️ **Retro Low-Poly Pixel Art**: 400×300 internal resolution, nearest-neighbor upscaled. All models built from pure BoxGeometry voxels — zero textures, zero external assets.
- 🏕️ **Roguelike Depth**: 22 Charms, 8 Relics, 10 random events, 5 Synergy combos, 5 camp upgrade trees, and specialization choices.
- 🖥️ **True Desktop Widget**: Frameless transparent window, system tray integration, global hotkeys, click-through idle mode, always-on-top option.
- 🌍 **12 Distinct Locations**: Japan, China, USA (Cyberpunk), UK, France, Germany, Italy, Denmark, Iceland, Thailand, Egypt, Greece.
- 🐟 **141 Unique Spirit Fish**: Procedural voxel models with size variants, spirit (shiny) variants, and trophy records.
- 📊 **Deep Progression**: Daily challenges, 15 achievements, login streaks, level 1-50 with location unlocks.
- 🎧 **Procedural Audio**: All sounds generated via Web Audio API — no audio files needed.
- 🌐 **Bilingual**: Full Chinese (简体中文) and English support.

|--------|-------|

### 📦 Installation

1. Download the appropriate build for your OS
2. Extract the archive
3. Run `Soul Sanctuary.exe` (Windows) / `Soul Sanctuary.app` (macOS) / `soul-sanctuary` (Linux)
4. The app starts minimized to tray — right-click the tray icon to begin!

## 🇨🇳 中文商店介绍

### 标语（短描述）

> 一款桌面钓鱼 Roguelike 小部件。在 12 个像素化的全球圣地中抛竿，收集 141 条精灵鱼，在茶歇间隙构筑终极护符 Synergy 组合。

---

### 详细描述

**灵魂避难所 · 深渊垂钓** 不是一款全屏游戏——它是你的**桌面伴侣**。一个小小的无边框窗口，悬浮在屏幕角落，在你工作时安静陪伴。按下 `Ctrl+Shift+F`，向精美的像素低多边形圣地抛一竿，体验 20 秒的张力博弈，然后回到工作中。

### 🎮 玩法概述

- **抛竿与收线**：单键张力小游戏。按住收线，松开泄力。掌握 6 种鱼行为模式（平稳/冲刺/慵懒/锯齿/深潜/跃水）。
- **探索 12 个圣地**：从京都的樱落神社到冰岛的极光教堂，从巴黎秋日塞纳河到霓虹赛博暗巷。每个圣地都是手工打造的体素微缩景观。
- **收集 141 条精灵鱼**：跨越 5 个稀有度（普通→神话）填充你的鱼鉴。每条鱼拥有独特的 3D 体素模型、行为 AI 和文化背景故事。
- **Roguelike 探险**：每次出发前从 22 张护符中挑选 3 张。叠加 Synergy 标签。获取永久遗物。应对随机事件。见好就收还是孤注一掷？

### ✨ 核心特色

- 🖼️ **复古低多边形像素风**：400×300 内部分辨率，邻近像素放大。所有模型由纯 BoxGeometry 体素块构建——零纹理、零外部资源。
- 🏕️ **Roguelike 深度**：22 种护符、8 件遗物、10 种随机事件、5 种 Synergy 组合、5 条营地升级线、专精选择。
- 🖥️ **真正的桌面小部件**：无边框透明窗口、系统托盘集成、全局快捷键、待机穿透点击、可置顶。
- 🌍 **12 个独特圣地**：日本、中国、美国（赛博朋克）、英国、法国、德国、意大利、丹麦、冰岛、泰国、埃及、希腊。
- 🐟 **141 条独特精灵鱼**：程序化体素模型，含体型变体、异色（闪光）变体、奖杯记录。
- 📊 **深度进度系统**：每日挑战、15 个成就、登录连击、1-50 级地点解锁。
- 🎧 **程序化音频**：所有音效通过 Web Audio API 生成——无需音频文件。
- 🌐 **双语支持**：完整简体中文与英文双语。
